# Move projects to a new computer

[Help](README.md)

A project is a folder, so moving it means copying that folder.

## On the old computer

1. Quit Helm (menu bar, notification area or tray icon › **Quit Helm**).
2. Open your **Helm** folder (the one that holds your projects; the default is a folder called **Helm** in your home folder, and **Settings › Appearance › New projects** shows yours).
3. Copy the project folders you want to a USB drive, a cloud drive or another computer on your network.
4. To bring your own library too (things you made in the Studio, and parts you installed), also copy Helm's own folder: `.helm` in your home folder. It's hidden; see [Mac](../install/mac.md#where-helm-keeps-things), [Windows](../install/windows.md#where-helm-keeps-things) or [Linux](../install/linux.md#where-helm-keeps-things) for how to find it.

## On the new computer

1. [Install Helm](../../README.md#install) and sign in to your agent ([Sign in or switch accounts](sign-in.md)).
2. Put the project folders in a **Helm** folder in your home folder, or anywhere you like.
3. (If you copied it) put the `.helm` folder in your home folder, before you open Helm for the first time.
4. Open Helm. For each project: **New project › A folder I already have** (or **All projects › Add a folder I already have**) and choose the folder. Helm opens it as it was.

Conversations you had are kept with Helm, not in the project, so they stay on the old computer unless you copied `.helm` too.
