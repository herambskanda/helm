# Share something to the Marketplace

[Help](README.md)

You can share a workspace, workbench, capability or helper you made. Heramb checks each one and lists it, so it takes a day or two to show up for others. You need a free GitHub account to send it.

1. Click **Marketplace** at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Share yours** (top right).
2. **Pick** what to share. The list shows things you made yourself. What you got from others is theirs to share.
3. **Checks.** Helm runs four, in plain words:
   - *Files well formed:* everything in it is readable and complete.
   - *What it may reach:* it says if the part runs code or uses the internet. Anything it reaches is listed for the people who get it.
   - *Its samples open:* its example opens properly.
   - *No secrets inside:* no passwords or keys are in the files.

   A check that fails says what to fix. Fix it (the Studio's agent can help) and run the checks again.
4. **Listing.** Write a name, one plain line about what it does, and who it's for. A preview shows how it will look.
5. **Ready to send.** Helm makes one package file (a `.zip`) and tells you where it is.
6. Open the [share form](https://github.com/herambskanda/helm/issues/new?template=share.yml), sign in to GitHub, write what it is and who it's for, and drag the `.zip` onto the form. Click **Submit new issue**.
7. In Helm, **Marketplace › Shared by you** lists it as *waiting*. When Heramb has checked and listed it, it says *listed*, and others can get it.

Your own copy stays yours. Sharing never changes it.
