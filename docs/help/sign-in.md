# Sign in or switch accounts

[Help](README.md)

Helm brings **Claude Code** and **Codex** with it. You sign in with your own account for each. Helm never sees your password: you sign in in your browser.

## Sign in the first time

1. On **Connect an agent**, click **Sign in with your Claude account** (Claude Code) or **Sign in with ChatGPT** (Codex).
2. Your browser opens. Sign in there and approve.
3. Come back to Helm. It shows you as signed in.

Skipped it? Open **Settings › Agents**. Each agent shows **Signed in as …** or **Not signed in**, with a **Sign in** button.

If you already use Claude Code on this computer, Helm finds you already signed in.

## Use a different account

1. Open **Settings › Agents** and find the agent.
2. Click **Sign in again** under its account.
3. In the browser, choose the other account. If the browser signs you straight back in as the first one, sign out of Claude (claude.ai) or ChatGPT in the browser first, then try again.

## Use the other agent

**Settings › Agents** sets the default agent for new conversations. In any conversation, **Agent pane › Setup** picks Claude Code or Codex for that conversation.

## It says "Not signed in" but I am

1. Quit Helm completely (menu bar, notification area or tray icon › **Quit Helm**) and open it again.
2. **Settings › Agents › Sign in again**.
3. Still stuck? [Report a problem](report-a-problem.md).

Nothing answering after signing in? [The agent doesn't answer](agent-doesnt-answer.md).
