# Get something from the Marketplace

[Help](README.md)

1. Click **Marketplace** at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Find it: scroll **Featured**, filter by kind (Templates, Workspaces, Workbenches, Capabilities, Agents & helpers) or who it's for, or type in the search box.
3. Click the item. Read **What's inside** (each part says *you have it* or *new*) and **What it may reach**.
4. Click **Get**. A box shows what it may reach: whether it runs code on your computer, or uses the internet. Click **Allow and install**, or **Not now**.
5. Do the next step, which depends on the kind:
   - **Template:** click **Start a new project with it**, name the project, and **Create project**.
   - **Workspace:** it's added to the project you have open. If none is open, open one first.
   - **Workbench:** choose the workspace to add it to. Or, from a workspace, **Add a kind of artifact…**.
   - **Capability:** keep it in your library, or add it to a workbench.
   - **Set** (several workbenches): **Keep in my library**, then add them to any workspace from **Add a kind of artifact…**.
6. **Marketplace › Installed** lists what you have, with updates first.

To take it away again, open it in **Marketplace › Installed** and remove it. Helm shows what that affects first.

No internet? Helm shows the last list it fetched, or the list built into the app.
